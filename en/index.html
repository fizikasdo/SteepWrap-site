School Research Project

Exploring new possibilities in packaging materials

SteepWrap is a school research project focused on developing and studying alternative packaging materials with the potential for biodegradation. We investigate the properties of experimental samples, conduct tests, and analyze the results.

About the Project

Our aim is to investigate how material composition and preparation methods may influence sample properties. We compare samples, document measurements, and consider the limitations of our research.

Research Areas

Material Composition

Studying components and their potential influence on the properties of the samples.

Experimental Testing

Comparing samples using selected measurements under documented test conditions.

Data Analysis

Organizing measurements, evaluating differences, and drawing conclusions based on evidence.

Our principle: conclusions must be supported by experimental results. We do not claim proven material advantages before the evidence has been reviewed.

Project Team

The research brings together scientific questions, experimental testing, data analysis, and communication. Meet the project team and learn about each member’s role on the Team page.
