The SteepWrap Team

SteepWrap is a research project focused on developing and investigating alternative packaging materials with potential for biodegradation. The team combines research, chemistry, and engineering.

Our Approach

We investigate material properties through experiments, document our results, and draw conclusions based on the available evidence. Our work emphasizes collaboration, engineering skills, and responsible scientific practice.
